About Me

Engineer, problem-solver, continuous learner

Hello! I'm Dylan.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Georgia Tech with a concentration in Manufacturing. I'm passionate about taking complex operations and making them measurable, repeatable, and faster.

My interests span process engineering, continuous improvement, and reliability — whether that's optimizing factory systems, building autonomous robots, or coordinating aircraft assembly. I love the intersection of hardware and data: instrumentation, dashboards, and data-driven decision-making.

I recently studied abroad at Georgia Tech Europe in Metz, France, which broadened my perspective on global engineering practices and cross-cultural collaboration.

What Drives Me

  • Making processes more efficient and reliable
  • Building things that actually work under real constraints
  • Using data to make better engineering decisions
  • Collaborating across teams to solve complex problems

Outside of engineering, I enjoy mentoring K-12 students through Curious Cardinal, helping them explore STEM subjects and navigate their educational journeys.
